Overview

Hi, welcome to the page for my roblox game dev project. This game was developed as a prototype rather than a full release, so this is more of my personal thoughts than proper documentation.

Project Introduction

This Roblox game was developed by a team of SJSU students for the WITH Cyber CSU Initiative. We were tasked with creating an educational game for K-6 audiences with the goals of:

  • Building safe internet habits
  • Introducing possible online dangers
  • Supporting a classroom learning environment
  • Testing the effectiveness of games as a medium of teaching

And so, we came up with UNTITLED GAME!! Actually, We had a name in development, but in my opinion, "Cyberguardians: The Quest for Safety" doesn't roll off the tongue. Instead, I will refer to it in name by a self-coined codename "Cyber Quest".
